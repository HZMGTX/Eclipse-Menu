# Changelog

## 1.3.0 — 2026-10-30

- **Space Games, a new category:** eight mini-games to play on your own, each with a scoreboard and a best score that's kept between sessions.
  - **Ring Run (A):** lay out a course of glowing rings and race through them.
  - **Asteroid Dodge:** rocks fly at your head, faster and faster. How long can you last?
  - **Comet Squash:** hit a glowing ball against a wall and keep the rally going.
  - **Simon Stars:** four stars light up in a pattern; touch them in the same order.
  - **Target Pop:** a 60-second round of popping targets around you.
  - **Gravity Golf:** hit a ball into a hole a few metres away, in as few strokes as you can.
  - **Constellation Connect:** join the stars of real constellations in order, against the clock.
  - **Star Bowling (B):** throw a glowing ball at ten pins.
- **5 new achievements:** Ring Racer, Survivor, Starry Memory, Hole in One and Strike!
- **Eclipse Website** button on the main page opens [menu.management/eclipse](https://www.menu.management/eclipse) in your browser.
- **Join Discord** now opens the Eclipse Menu server.
- Surprise Me, Turn Off All and Mod of the Day work with Space Games too.

As always, every game acts on you alone and sends nothing over the network.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-23

- **Space Sky (15), a new category:** Ringed Planet, Twin Moons, Space Station, Satellites, Comet Flyby, Galaxy Band, Rocket Launches, Nebula Sky, Binary Stars, Asteroid Belt, Spiral Galaxy, Pulsar Star, Starship Fleet, Black Hole Horizon and Space Whale. Big things in your sky that only you can see.

- **12 more Wrist Tools:** Lap Timer (A drops a start gate), Waypoint (B), Jump Height, Air Time, Room Card, Tap Counter, G-Force Meter, Speed Graph, World Clock, Spirit Level, Head Height and Room Timer.
- **4 more Space Movement mods:** Wall Slide, Moon Zone (X drops a low-gravity dome), Comet Jab (hold B and punch) and Star Stroke (hold A and swim through the air).
- **Mod of the Day:** a different Eclipse mod every day, on the dashboard and in Eclipse Mods.
- **Random Theme** in Menu Settings picks one of Eclipse's space themes for you.
- **3 new themes:** Solar Flare, Moonstone and Dark Matter.
- **4 new achievements:** Sky Watcher, Speedster, Lap Legend and Juggler.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-23

Three new categories on the Eclipse tab, and more to the menu itself.

- **Space Toys (10):** Mini Solar System, Planet Builder (both grips), Kite, Yo-yo (RG), Paper Planes (RT), Magic 8-Ball (shake your left hand), Space Dice (X), Glow Sticks (LG), Echo Ghost and Meteor Defense (B). Only you can see them.
- **Wrist Tools (8):** Battery Meter, Coordinates, Hand Speed, Measuring Tape, Mic Meter, Ping Alarm, Break Reminder and Countdown Timer (X).
- **Space Movement (5):** Charge Jump (LT), Zipline (RT, ride with RG), Air Strafe, Orbit Mode (RG) and Gravity Well (Y).
- **Recent Mods** on the Home tab: the last 12 mods you switched, newest first. It is remembered between sessions.
- **Surprise Me** in Eclipse Mods and Space Toys turns on something you haven't tried, and every new category has a Turn Off All.
- **5 new themes:** Pulsar, Event Horizon, Mars Dust, Neptune and Cosmic Latte, the first light theme.
- **4 new achievements:** Planet Maker, Meteor Defender, Zipliner and Toy Box.

As before, every one of these acts on you alone and sends nothing over the network.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-10-23

- **Glowing edges:** the rims, corner brackets, header line and the switches of mods you have on now glow, with a soft halo around them.
- **Shiny tiles:** the menu's tiles catch the light, and give off a little of their own colour so they still show in dark maps.
- Both can be turned off in Menu Settings: **Disable Eclipse Glow** and **Disable Eclipse Shine**.
- **Coming from Nova?** Eclipse offers to bring over the mods you had on the first time it finds Nova's settings, and Settings → Import Nova Settings does it any time.
- The leftover Poison and Seralyth names are gone. The in-game board, the Console banner and the DLL now say Eclipse Menu by HZMGTX. The credits are unchanged.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-22

The first release of Eclipse Menu, remade from Nova Menu.

- **A new look:** a header bar with the Eclipse logo, mod rows with on/off switches, corner brackets, rounded 3D tiles, buttons that push in, a springy open and a star pointer.
- **Four layouts:** Eclipse Panel (tabs down the sides), Orbit Wheel, Holo Grid and Classic, picked in Menu Settings → Change Menu Layout.
- **Eclipse Mods:** 40 new space mods (sky, stars, planets, space movement, wrist tools and toys). Every one only affects you or what you see.
- **Dashboard and Quick Wheel:** live FPS, ping and room info, and a wheel of your favourite mods on your left stick click.
- **Themes and sounds:** 10 space themes and 3 new sound packs.
- **And more:** card notifications, 4 pointer styles, an intro, 8 new achievements and a What's New page.
- **Credits:** updated with Poison Menu's current and former owners.

Downloads are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/HZMGTX/Eclipse-Menu/releases).
