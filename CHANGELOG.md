# Changelog

## 1.0.1 — 2026-10-23

- **Glowing edges:** the rims, corner brackets, header line and the switches of mods you have on now glow, with a soft halo around them.
- **Shiny tiles:** the menu's tiles catch the light, and give off a little of their own colour so they still show in dark maps.
- Both can be turned off in Menu Settings: **Disable Eclipse Glow** and **Disable Eclipse Shine**.
- **Coming from Nova?** Eclipse offers to bring over the mods you had on the first time it finds Nova's settings, and Settings → Import Nova Settings does it any time.
- The leftover Poison and Seralyth names are gone. The in-game board, the Console banner and the DLL now say Eclipse Menu by HZMGTX. The credits are unchanged.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-22

The first release of Eclipse Menu, remade from Nova Menu.

- **A new look:** a header bar with the Eclipse logo, mod rows with on/off switches, corner brackets, rounded 3D tiles, buttons that push in, a springy open and a star pointer.
- **Four layouts:** Eclipse Panel (tabs down the sides), Orbit Wheel, Holo Grid and Classic, picked in Menu Settings → Change Menu Layout.
- **Eclipse Mods:** 40 new space mods (sky, stars, planets, space movement, wrist tools and toys). Every one only affects you or what you see.
- **Dashboard and Quick Wheel:** live FPS, ping and room info, and a wheel of your favourite mods on your left stick click.
- **Themes and sounds:** 10 space themes and 3 new sound packs.
- **And more:** card notifications, 4 pointer styles, an intro, 8 new achievements and a What's New page.
- **Credits:** updated with Poison Menu's current and former owners.

Downloads are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/HZMGTX/Eclipse-Menu/releases).
